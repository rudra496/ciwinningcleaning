FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a clean cost?
Our starting prices are shown on each service — for example, regular domestic cleaning from £18/hr and end of tenancy from £120 per property. Your exact quote depends on property size, condition, location and what's included. Submit the booking form for a free, no-obligation quote.
Do you clean homes as well as businesses?
Yes — CI Winning Cleaning serves all facility types: houses, flats and apartments, offices, shops, holiday lets, student accommodation and communal areas. Domestic and commercial cleaning are handled by dedicated teams.
What's included in an end of tenancy clean?
A full-property clean covering every room: oven and appliance cleaning, bathroom descaling, inside cupboards and wardrobes, internal windows, and move-in/move-out preparation. Check the end of tenancy service page for the complete checklist.
Do I need to provide cleaning products or equipment?
No — our teams arrive with professional products and equipment as standard. If you prefer specific products (for example, eco-friendly or allergy-sensitive), mention it in your booking notes and we'll accommodate where possible.
Can I book a regular weekly or fortnightly cleaner?
Absolutely — choose your frequency in the booking form (weekly, fortnightly or one-off) and we'll set up a schedule with the same standards every visit. Regular customers get priority scheduling.
How do I get a quote?
Fill in the booking form with your property type, number of bedrooms, postcode and preferred date. We'll confirm availability in your area and send a free quote — no obligation, no hidden fees.
What is the Bonanza offer?
Until 30 November, one-off cleaning of houses is just £100 (Bonanza price). Book through the booking form and mention the Bonanza — your free quote confirms the exact scope before we start.
