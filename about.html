Our Story

Built on Sparkling Results

Fly Cleaner is a professional cleaning company serving homes and businesses across the UK. We do one thing exceptionally well: leave every property genuinely spotless.

Cleaning is one of those services where "fine" isn't good enough. A rushed end-of-tenancy clean can cost a tenant their deposit. A dusty office undermines a business. So we built Fly Cleaner differently — every service has a defined checklist, every quote is free and transparent, and every clean is measured against the same standard: would we be happy to walk in ourselves?

From regular domestic cleaning and deep cleans to offices, carpets and Airbnb changeovers — one trusted team, seven specialist services.

  • Checklist-driven cleans — you always know what's included, and we always deliver it.
  • Free quotes first — clear, honest pricing before any work begins.
  • All facility types — houses, flats, offices, shops, holiday lets and communal areas.
